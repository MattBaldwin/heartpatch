Long Night After screens

Captures of the Long Night client, for checking against the owner-approved mockups (v5). The client only draws what the server sends, so states the dev routes can't reach by day use stubbed server replies. Each of those shots is marked “Stubbed” in purple, with what was changed.

Captured
Playwright in Chromium (iphone-chromium project) against the local dev servers, with the dev HUD hidden. Sessions can’t run WebKit.
Safe areas
iPhone 15 shots emulate real insets with CDP Emulation.setSafeAreaInsetsOverride (upright: top 59, bottom 34). The SE has none. No iPhone 15 sideways shots were taken this round.
Quality tier
Each caption names the tier at capture. The container draws WebGL in software, so the map tier falls to low on some shots.
Dev clock
Map time is pinned to late morning. Any day-lit sky or “tonight!” chip after a strike comes from the dev route, not the client. At night the chip reads “🌙 Long Night”.
Not shown
iPhone 15 sideways. The patch-mate chooser on iPhone 15 and SE sideways.
