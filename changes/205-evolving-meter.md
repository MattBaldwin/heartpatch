title: See how close a squishy is to evolving
area: squishies
---

A squishy's care sheet now has a sparkly Evolving bar under its level, filling up toward its next form. After a battle, the results show how much closer each squishy got, like "+8% toward evolving!". When the bar is full, one more battle does it!
**Try it:** open a squishy's care sheet, then win a battle and watch its bar fill.
