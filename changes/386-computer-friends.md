---
title: Computer friends can move in next door
area: other
---

Patch owners can switch on Computer friends: friendly Keepers run by the game who fill empty homes until real friends join. They always wear a plum key badge, so you can tell who's who, and they wave goodbye to make room when a real friend moves in. Your neighbours claim land, light fires, trade and battle too.
**Try it:** open your patch's settings and turn Computer friends On.
