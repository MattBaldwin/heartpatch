---
title: Peek upstairs and downstairs
area: home
---

Once your house has an upstairs, you see one floor at a time, so the Kitchen never hides your cozy Snug. Tap Upstairs to visit the Kitchen, and Downstairs to come back. On a phone, drag sideways to see the rest of your house.
**Try it:** in a house with a Kitchen, tap ⬆️ Upstairs, then ⬇️ Downstairs.
