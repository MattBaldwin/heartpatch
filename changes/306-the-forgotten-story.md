---
title: The forgotten story
area: land
---

Every grey Hollow tile hides a torn page 📜, found the first time you search there. Find them all and the broken thing in the heart of the Hollow wakes up: read the clues and put the Hollow's last day back in order. Get it wrong and the ink shifts to a brand new day! Telling it right gives you a lore page or a little Heartdust.
**Try it:** search every tile of a grey Hollow, then walk up to the broken thing on its middle tile and tap Fix.
