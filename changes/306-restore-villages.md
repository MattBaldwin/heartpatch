---
title: Bring a grey Hollow back
area: land
---

Put the pieces back on a grey Hollow with Timber, Stone and a little Heartdust. Finish a tile and it's yours; finish them all and the Hollow gets its own Heart Seed, a second home nobody can ever take. Then you get to name it!
**Try it:** by day, tap a grey Hollow tile next to your land, tap Restore, then tap Restore a piece.
