---
title: Home, chat, squishies and team fit sideways
area: other
---

Turn your phone on its side and Home, Chat, your Squishies list and your Team now fit much better. Home takes less of the screen, and the Squishies list, the Team and Chat sit in two columns so you can see more at once. The 💛 button is easier to tap too.
**Try it:** rotate your phone, then open Home, 🐾 Squishies, or ⚔️ Jobs & team.
