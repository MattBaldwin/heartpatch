---
title: Woken doors glow
area: land
---

When a friend wakes a sleeping door in the Crooked House, the camera swings over to it and a cosy glow lights the doorway and the floor at its foot, in the friend's colour, with a gentle pulse. The "who can help" bubble now says the friend needs to be on your team.
**Try it:** in the Parlour, tap the cold fireplace, pick a Fire friend from your team, and watch the light fill the doorway.
