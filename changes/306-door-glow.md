---
title: Woken doors light up
area: land
---

When a friend wakes a sleeping door in the Crooked House, the camera swings over to it and the door gets a glowing outline in the friend’s colour, with embers, leaves, droplets or star sparkles drifting up from it. The "who can help" bubble now says the friend needs to be on your team.
**Try it:** in the Parlour, tap the cold fireplace, pick a Fire friend from your team, and watch the door glow with embers.
