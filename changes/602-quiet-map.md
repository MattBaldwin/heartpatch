No more 🧺 bubbles piling up! Every gatherer now stands on its tile as itself, small and cozy. Tap a tile to see who's working there and how they feel. Only your own squishies that need you get a tiny 🌧️ or 💤.
**Try it:** send a squishy to gather, then find it on the map and tap its tile.
