Spend your Heart Coins at the post on Heart Charm packs, Potion crates and a Rare Lure. Hold a lure and Find a squishy glows, because your next wild find leans rarer!
**Try it:** open a trading post, tap the 🛍️ Shop tab, then buy a Rare Lure and look for the glow.
