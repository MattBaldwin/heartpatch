---
title: Buildings fill their home tiles
area: home
---

Your Den, Meadow and Training Grounds now sit in the middle of their tile and fill it, just like buildings out on the land. The tile's tree, rock or pumpkin patch shrinks to a small pile by the edge, and you can still gather from it.
**Try it:** open Home, look at a tile with a habitat on it, then tap the small pile at its edge on the map to gather.
