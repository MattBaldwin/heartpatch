---
title: Dens and Meadows fill their home tiles
area: home
---

Your Ember Den and Cozy Meadow now sit in the middle of their tile and fill it, just like buildings out on the land. The tile's tree, rock or pumpkin patch shrinks to a small pile by the edge, and you can still gather from it.
**Try it:** open the map at your home, find a tile with a Den or Meadow, and tap the small pile at its edge to gather.
