---
title: The Hollow Man costume looks like the Hollow Man
area: other
---

The Mythic Hollow Man costume now turns your Keeper into the real Hollow Man: tall and thin in a hooded cloak with a ragged hem, long arms and soft glowing gold eyes, all with the shimmery Mythic finish. It takes your Keeper's place while you wear it.
**Try it:** wear the Hollow Man costume in your wardrobe, then look for your Keeper on the map.
