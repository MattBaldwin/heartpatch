---
title: The Hollow Man costume looks like the Hollow Man
area: other
---

The Mythic Hollow Man costume now turns your Keeper into the real Hollow Man. He's tall and thin in a hooded cloak with a ragged hem, with long arms and soft glowing gold eyes, plus a pearly Mythic shimmer. He takes your Keeper's place while you wear him.
**Try it:** if you find the Hollow Man costume, wear it in your wardrobe and look for your Keeper on the map.
