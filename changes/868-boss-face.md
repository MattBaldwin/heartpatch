title: Look a BIG squishy in the eye
area: battles

On phones with a notch, a BIG squishy's face now stays in the picture for the whole fight, even when it powers up or gets bonked. Your team ducks a little lower so everyone fits!
**Try it:** start a BIG squishy fight on your iPhone and watch its eyes. No more peeking from behind the name sign!
