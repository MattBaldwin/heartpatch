title: The Rekindled set

Hollows hide the glowing Rekindled set. Find all five pieces to be a Rekindler!
They hide Joy Embers and Heart Petals too, for two new recipes: the Joy Lantern, a fire that shines extra far, and cozy Heart Tea.
**Try it:** search a Hollow by day and see what glows.
