---
title: Big squishies sit down, twirl and say yes
area: battles
---

When the BIG squishy gets all tuckered out, it sits down for a little Zzz. If your team gets tuckered out, it twirls (gently!) and tells you the day it leaves, so you can come back stronger. A friend who says yes gets a happy sparkle, and the warmer it likes you, the pinker it blushes and the more hearts pop out.
**Try it:** beat a BIG squishy, then ask it to be your friend. Your answer gets hearts and a blush!
