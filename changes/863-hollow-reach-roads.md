---
title: Roads reach Hollows and trading posts
area: land
---

On a Wild Isle, land your road, bridge or ferry joins to home now counts for Hollows and trading posts, so you can explore, restore and trade there! If your land by one is cut off, the panel now says so.
**Try it:** on a Wild Isle, lay a road to land next to a grey Hollow, tap one of its tiles and press Explore.
