---
title: Night comes at 7, and the Hollow Man gets bolder
area: land
---

Night now falls at 7 PM. Each night you're on a patch the Hollow Man gets a little bolder, and on land with no fire light he can take a squishy and make the land go wild again. Keep every bit of your land in a Hearthfire's glow!
**Try it:** light a fire near every bit of your land before 7 PM.
