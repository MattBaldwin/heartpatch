---
title: The Long Night
area: land
---

Once a week the whole patch stays up for the Long Night. The Hollow Man looks extra hard, so fuel your fires! If the patch holds, Juniper's Gap opens for a day and every Keeper gets one special look inside.
**Try it:** ask your patch owner to check the Long Night day in the patch's settings (it starts on Saturday), then watch for "🌑 Long Night" over the map.
