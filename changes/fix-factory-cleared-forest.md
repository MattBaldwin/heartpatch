---
title: Factories on cleared land
area: land
---

Cleared land takes its own factory now: a Woodcutter's Hut on cleared forest, a Water Well on a cleared lake, and all the rest. On land with a spot, Build shows which one goes there once you clear it.
**Try it:** clear a forest homestead with your Axe, then tap Build and put up a Woodcutter's Hut.
