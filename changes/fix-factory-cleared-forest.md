---
title: Build on land you cleared
area: home
---

Cleared land takes its own make-it-yourself building now: a Woodcutter's Hut on cleared forest, a Water Well on a cleared lake, and all the rest. On land with a spot, Build shows which one goes there once you clear it.
**Try it:** clear a forest homestead with your Axe, then tap 🔨 Build and put up a Woodcutter's Hut.
