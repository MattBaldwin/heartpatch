---
title: See when your tries come back
area: land
---

Out of claim tries? Wild land now says "New tries in 3h 20m 🌙" instead of "Come back tomorrow!". Land you just fought over says when it's rested, and a squishy past its full-XP wins says "Full XP again in 5h".
**Try it:** use all 5 claim tries, then tap wild land next to yours.
