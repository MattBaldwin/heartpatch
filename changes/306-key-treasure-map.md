Bring a Hollow back and you get an old treasure map of your patch, with an ✕ where the squishies hid the cellar key. Its tile glows gold on your map, just for you. Make that land yours, explore it, and dig up the glinting spot!
**Try it:** open your Bag and tap Treasure map 🗺️, then tap Show me on the map.
