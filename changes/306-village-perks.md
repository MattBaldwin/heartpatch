---
title: Hollows wake up
area: land
---

A Hollow you win now does its job! A Lantern Tower lights the land around it every night, a Workshop gives your Factory one more batch, a Watchtower makes your guards fight 2 levels stronger, and an Old Well or a Bakery leaves you a gift every morning.
**Try it:** tap the Heart Seed in the middle of your Hollow, then tap Collect today’s gift.
