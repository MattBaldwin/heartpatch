---
title: Nine more squishies to find
area: squishies
---

Nine new squishies are out in the wild: a belly-sliding penguin, a piglet with a stub tail, a bear cub that hugs a big red heart, a potato with a sprout, a sheep made of cloud, a firefly, a dragon that is also a dragonfruit, a rubber duck that blows bubbles and an anglerfish scared of its own light. Some only come out in daylight, and some only at night.
**Try it:** visit the lake after dark, or the hills on a sunny day, and see who turns up.
