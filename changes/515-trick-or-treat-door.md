---
title: Knock, knock!
area: land
---

Trick-or-treating now happens at the door! Knock, and the door swings open. Sometimes a silly ghost says "Boo!" first, and then the treat comes anyway. Your Keeper and Partner stand on the porch, and a costume shows how it looks on them.
**Try it:** tap a friend's Heart Seed, tap Trick or treat!, then tap Knock!
