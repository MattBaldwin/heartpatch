---
title: Steady and Big moves
area: battles
---

Every boop is now 💥 Big or 🛡️ Steady. Big hits hard but leaves you wide open. Steady is lighter, puts you on guard, and is gentle: it never tuckers out a wild squishy, so it's perfect before a Heart Charm.
**Try it:** tap Find a squishy, tap a 🛡️ Steady move until the wild squishy is holding on by a whisker, then tap Use Heart Charm.
