title: Eight new squishies to find
area: squishies
---

Eight new squishies are out in the wild: a glowing moth, a pink shooting star, a frog, a sleepy turtle, a garden gnome, a snow bunny, a hot pepper and a snow person. Look for them in meadows, hills, mountains, lakes and the forest.
**Try it:** befriend wild squishies in different places and see who turns up in your collection.
