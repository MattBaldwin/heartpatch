Got Pies or Frosty Treats? Pick one right above Feed. The best one for your squishy wears a gold Best tag, and an extra-yummy feed gets a big burst of hearts! 🥧🍧

**Try it:** Open a squishy's care, tap Pies (or Frosty for a Frost squishy), then tap Feed.
