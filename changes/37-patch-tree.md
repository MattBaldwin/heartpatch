---
title: The Patch Tree
area: land
---

Your patch has a Patch Tree by a trading post! 🎄 Every December gift you send a patch-mate hangs an ornament, and your hearts of warmth grow on their card. Hang 12 together to light the star! ⭐
**Try it:** Send a patch-mate a gift in December, then find the tree by the trading post at night to see the ornaments glow.
