---
title: Woken doors sparkle
area: land
---

When a friend wakes a sleeping door in the Crooked House, the camera swings over to it and a soft glow in the friend’s colour pops up on both sides of the doorway, with embers, leaves, droplets or star sparkles drifting up beside it. The "who can help" bubble says the friend needs to be on your team.
**Try it:** in the Parlour, tap the cold fireplace, pick a Fire friend from your team, and watch the glow and embers on both sides of the door.
