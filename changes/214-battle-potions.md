title: Battle potions
area: battles
---

Make Brave Brew, Cozy Cocoa or Hearty Soup in your recipe book, then tap "Use item" in a battle. Each one also puts a sparkle shield on your squishy that softens the next bump.
**Try it:** make a Brave Brew from 2 Treats and 2 Stone, drink it in a wild battle, and look for ⚔️+ on your squishy's pill.
