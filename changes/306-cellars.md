title: The hidden cellar
area: land
---

Restore a whole village and its squishies hand you a map! Follow it to the tile where they hid the cellar key, dig it up, then open the secret cellar under the village square for extra-good finds. Wild squishies also come back to restored village tiles that have gone wild again.
**Try it:** restore every tile of a village, tap Show me on the map, then Dig for the key 🔑.
