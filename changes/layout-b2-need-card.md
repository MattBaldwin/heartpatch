---
title: Sparkles peek out from the tool card
area: land
---

When a spot needs a tool you don't have yet, its little card now scoots out of the way so you can still see (and tap!) the spot's sparkle, even with your phone on its side.
**Try it:** turn your phone sideways on a river tile, walk up to a swirly eddy without a Net, and spot the sparkle beside the card.
