---
title: You can see the BIG squishy fight now
area: battles
---

On iPhone, BIG squishy fights showed only dark sky. Now you can see the whole fight: your team, the BIG squishy and the arena. The biggest BIG squishies also lost their stripy streaks and wear a rainbow glow instead.
**Try it:** tap a BIG squishy on your map, hit Let's go! and watch your team bump it.
