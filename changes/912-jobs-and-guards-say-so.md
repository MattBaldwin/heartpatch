---
title: Jobs say what happened, right where you tapped
area: squishies
---

If a squishy can't take a job, the reason now pops up in its own row, and sleepy homesteads and land being cleared aren't offered at all. Guards show up by name on the land they protect, and the guard picker says what each squishy is busy with.
**Try it:** tap your land, pick guards, and look for "🛡️ … is on watch here".
