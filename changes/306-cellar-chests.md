The secret cellar is now a cozy stone vault lit by candles, with a row of treasure chests along the back wall. Pop each one open, then open the big chest in the middle for a giant pile of Heartdust and Glimmer and a one-of-a-kind keepsake to wear. Each kind of Hollow hides its own!
**Try it:** go down to a Hollow's cellar and tap Open by a chest 🗝️.
