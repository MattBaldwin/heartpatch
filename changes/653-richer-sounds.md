title: Treasure sounds like treasure
area: other

Opening a chest now builds up to a sparkly ta-da! Waking a door, finding a key, fixing a room, a new friend, evolving and winning all got bigger, shinier sounds, and the little taps and boops are softer and cozier.
**Try it:** open the bonus room's chest in the Crooked House, with sound on.
