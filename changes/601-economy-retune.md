---
title: Warehouse costs less to keep
area: home
---

The Warehouse now costs 20 💛 each morning instead of 30, and you can sell up to 150 💛 of extras at the post each day instead of 120. Keeping a whole patch of buildings running is easier now.
**Try it:** build a Warehouse, then open the Sell tab at a trading post. The daily 💛 bar goes up to 150.
