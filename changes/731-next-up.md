---
title: A Next up chip shows your nearest goals
area: other
---

A little chip at the bottom of your patch shows the goal you're closest to, and taps open your three nearest goals with a button for each. On a patch that's only you, it says why milestones are waiting and puts your invite code one tap away.
**Try it:** tap Next up at the bottom of your patch, or open Adventure and tap Next up.
