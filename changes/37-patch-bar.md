---
title: The patch bar
area: battles
---

On Christmas Eve a thin line under the BIG squishy's name fills up as everyone on your patch chips in, and turns gold when it's full! The patch's card shows ❄ and how full it is, and after your fight a "Chipped!" sticker says how much you added.
**Try it:** fight the Christmas Eve squishy and watch the line under its name grow.
