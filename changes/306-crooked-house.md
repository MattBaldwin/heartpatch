---
title: The Crooked House
area: land
---

At the end of the far road, a wonky old house waits just for you. Walk its dark rooms by lantern light, put crooked things right to bring the colour back, and find the keys to open new doors! Some doors are asleep: a Fire, Leaf, Water or Light friend on your team can wake them, and a treasure chest waits behind each one.
**Try it:** take the far road from a trading post, then tap Go inside 🕯️.
