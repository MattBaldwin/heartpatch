---
title: The Crooked House
area: land
---

At the end of the far road, a wonky old house waits just for you. Walk its dark rooms by lantern light, put crooked things right to bring the colour back, and find the keys to open new doors! Friendly echoes drift through the halls: Tiptoe past them, or they'll Peekaboo you! 👻 Relight the lamps in the Library and the Music Room, gather the house's story scrap by scrap, and open the big chest in the attic for the Lantern Keeper's Cloak.
**Try it:** take the far road from a trading post, then tap Go inside 🕯️.
