---
title: The Crooked House
area: land
---

At the end of the far road, a wonky old house waits just for you. Walk its dark rooms by lantern light, put crooked things right to bring the colour back, and find the keys to open new doors!
Tiptoe past the friendly echoes (or get a Peekaboo! 👻), relight the reading lamps and the music box, and gather the house's story to open the attic's big chest.
**Try it:** take the far road from a trading post, then tap Go inside 🕯️.
