---
title: Buildings that make things by themselves
area: home
---

Build a Woodcutter's Hut, Stone Quarry, Greenhouse and more on your homesteads. Each one makes its land's resource all by itself, much faster than a squishy, and drops it in your Bag when you come back. They cost a few 💛 each morning, and you pick which ones keep running when coins are short.
**Try it:** tap one of your homesteads, tap 🔨 Build, and build the one that matches its land.
