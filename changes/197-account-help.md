---
title: Help getting back in
area: account
---

Forgot your name? This device now remembers who plays on it: tap your name, then type your password. Save your recovery code as a picture, and make a new one in Settings any time. You can also pick a grown-up helper who can see your name and reset your password if you forget it.
**Try it:** log out, then tap Log in. Or open Settings and tap "Add a helper".
