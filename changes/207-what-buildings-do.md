---
title: See what each building does
area: home
---

The build menu now says what each building is for, with little chips for what it does: how far a fire keeps squishies safe, who grows faster in a habitat, and how many squishies a Training Grounds trains. Building a fire on your land shows the same.
**Try it:** open Home, tap Build, and read the rows. Then tap a tile of your land and Build a fire.
