---
title: Move buttons tell you what they do
area: battles
---

Every move button now says what kind of move it is: a Boop, a Heal, a Shield and more. It has a little picture, a few easy words, and shows if it's strong or weak against who you're facing.
**Try it:** start a battle and look at the move buttons. Tap the ⓘ to read more about each one.
