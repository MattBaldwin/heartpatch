---
title: Relight the path
area: land
---

Grey Hollows have broken things to fix: a music box, a mural or a fountain! Turn and slide the pieces so the light reaches every lantern, but keep it away from the echoes 👻. Run out of moves and the Hollow shuffles a new path. Lighting one gives you a lore page 📜 or a little Heartdust.
**Try it:** explore a grey Hollow tile, walk up to the broken thing and tap Fix.
