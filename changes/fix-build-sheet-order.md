---
title: The right building comes first
area: home
---

On cleared land, 🔨 Build now shows the building that goes there first, like the Woodcutter's Hut on cleared forest. Building names also wrap neatly now, never in the middle of a word.
**Try it:** clear a forest homestead, then tap 🔨 Build. The Woodcutter's Hut is the first card.
