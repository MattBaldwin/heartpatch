---
title: Explore your land
area: land
---

Walk your Keeper around your own land and search every sparkly spot: flip rocks, shake trees, and craft a Shovel, Net, Rope or Lantern to dig, scoop, climb and peek into caves. Explore every spot and the land joins your home, so its gatherers bring back a little extra.
**Try it:** tap a tile you own, press Explore, then walk up to a sparkle and press the big button.
