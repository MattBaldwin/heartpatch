---
title: Explore your land
area: land
---

Walk your Keeper around your own land and search every sparkle: flip rocks, shake trees, and craft a Shovel, Net, Rope or Lantern for the trickier spots. Explore a whole tile next to home and it joins your home!
**Try it:** tap a tile you own, press Explore, then walk up to a sparkle and press the big button.
