---
title: Everyone fits in the house
area: home
---

Your squishies now always fit on screen when you look at your house, even the wide, bouncy ones that used to peek off the edge. The little "Can move back in" note fits on small phones, and the map buttons stay tucked away while your Squishies list is open.
**Try it:** open your house on a phone and look at every squishy, then tap 🐾 on the map and check the bottom corners.
