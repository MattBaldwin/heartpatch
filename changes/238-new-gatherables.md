---
title: Water, Greens and Ice
area: land
---

Lakes have little wells now. Squishy gatherers pick Greens on meadows, chip Ice on mountains and fetch Water from lakes. Cook Greens into Treats, or freeze Water into Ice (a Frost squishy on your team makes it twice as fast)!
**Try it:** send a squishy to gather on a meadow you own, then open your recipe book and cook 2 Greens into Treats.
