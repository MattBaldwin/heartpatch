---
title: Water, Greens and Ice
area: land
---

Lakes have little wells now, meadows grow Greens and mountains sparkle with Ice. Fill your bag! You can also freeze Water into Ice at home, and a Frost squishy on your team makes it twice as fast.
**Try it:** claim a lake next to your land, tap it and press Gather Water, then open your recipe book and freeze 3 Water into Ice.
