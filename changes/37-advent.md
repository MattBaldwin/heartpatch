It's December, so there's a present waiting every day! 🎁 Open "Presents" in My Home to see the whole calendar, peek at what today's might hold, and open it to see what's inside. Missed a day? You can open one you missed, once a day.

**Try it:** Open My Home on your patch in December and tap Presents.
