For kids and grown-ups

Heartpatch keeps your secrets safe

Heartpatch is a cozy squishy game for families. We keep as little about you as we can. Here's all of it.

🎒 What we keep

  • A made-up name. The one you pick to play, never your real name. A filter says no to unkind names, phone numbers and addresses.
  • Your password, scrambled. It's stored as an Argon2id hash, so nobody can read it. Not even us.
  • The year you were born. Just the year, to tell kids from grown-ups.
  • Your device's time zone, like "America/Chicago", so each day in the game starts at your midnight.
  • Which code you signed up with, and who made it, so your family stays together.
  • Your game: your squishies, land, Keeper, outfits and progress, and the patches you play in.
  • Quick messages. Players pick them from a list; nobody can type their own. Each patch keeps only its latest 30.
  • One cookie, to keep you logged in. That's all it does.
  • Server logs. Our server notes each visit, with an internet address, to keep things running and stop spam. Old logs are cleared by themselves.

On your device, the game also remembers the names that log in there (so you can tap yours) and settings like sound. "Not you?" on the log in screen forgets the names.

🚫 What we never ask for

  • No email, real name, address or phone number
  • No photos, camera, microphone or location
  • No typed chat with other players
  • No ads, and nothing to buy with real money
  • No tracking or analytics. The game only talks to our own server.
  • We never sell or share anything about you.

💗 For grown-ups

  • Want an account deleted? Ask Matt to delete it, along with its game. Our nightly backups keep a copy for up to 14 days, then it's gone.
  • Forgot a password? Use the recovery code from sign-up, or ask your patch owner or Matt.
  • Questions? Ask Matt, the grown-up who gave you your family code.
