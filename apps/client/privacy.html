What Heartpatch keeps about you

Heartpatch is a cozy squishy game for families. We keep as little about you as we can. Here's all of it.

🎒 What we keep

  • A made-up name. The one you pick to play, never your real name. A filter says no to unkind names, phone numbers and addresses.
  • Your password, scrambled. It's stored as an Argon2id hash, so nobody can read it. Not even us. Your recovery code is scrambled the same way.
  • The year you were born. Just the year, to tell kids from grown-ups.
  • Your device's time zone, like "America/Chicago", so each day in the game starts at your midnight.
  • Which code you signed up with, and who made it, so your family stays together.
  • Your game: your squishies and the names you give them, your home and land, Keeper, outfits and progress, the patches you play in, and any grown-up helper you asked to help you.
  • Quick messages. Players pick them from a list; nobody can type their own. The chat shows each patch's latest 30. The game's diary also notes who sent which one, and keeps that for a while.
  • One cookie, to keep you logged in. That's all it does.
  • Server logs. Our server notes each visit, with an internet address, to keep things running and stop spam. Old logs are cleared by themselves.
  • A slow-game note. If the game runs very slowly, it tells our server how fast it ran, so we can fix it. Just numbers, and your account's id.

On your device, the game also remembers the names that log in there (so you can tap yours) and settings like sound, and what you've already seen. "Not you?" on the log in screen forgets the names.

🚫 What we never ask for

  • No email, real name, address or phone number
  • No photos, camera, microphone or location
  • No typed chat with other players
  • No ads, and nothing to buy with real money
  • No tracking or analytics. The game only talks to our own server.
  • We never sell or share anything about you.

💗 For grown-ups

  • Want an account deleted? Ask Matt to delete it, along with its game. Our nightly backups still hold a copy for up to 14 days, and any older backup Matt has saved stays private.
  • Forgot a password? Use the recovery code from sign-up, or ask your patch owner, your grown-up helper or Matt.
  • Questions? Ask Matt, the grown-up who gave you your family code.
