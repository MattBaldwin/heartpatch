## The Christmas Eve BIG squishy: **Snowbundle**

A huge, round, sleepy snow-heap of a squishy that walks in on Christmas Eve to see the patch's lights. It is not a fighter by nature: it is a friendly giant who wants everyone to smile and gets a little sparkly-sneezy.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | **Snowbundle** (BIG). Tiny form: **Bundlebit**. Marker: "A BIG snowy squishy!" |
| **Silhouette** | A fat two-tier snowman-shape with no neck: a wide round body and a smaller round head sunk into it, so it reads as one big **gumdrop on a pillow** from far away. Short stubby arms, no legs (it rests on a wide round base and waddles). It is the widest and the roundest boss, roughly three times the height of a Puddlepuff, with a clear shape even at map size. |
| **Colours** | Body: soft **ice-blue** (`~#d9eefc`) with a **snow-white** belly and head cap (`~#ffffff`). **Accent:** warm **cranberry** (`~#d9485a`) on the scarf and hat. A tiny sparkle of warm **gold** is allowed on the star only. Two colours plus one accent, so it stays distinct from the pink and brown boss lines. |
| **Face** | Two big dot eyes set low and wide, tiny rosy blush discs, a small sleepy smile. Eyes half-lidded when idle, wide and sparkling when it is happy. No teeth, no brow, never fierce. |
| **Signature prop** | A **knitted nightcap with a lit star** at the tip, and a scarf long enough to trail on the ground. The star is the only emissive part: it pulses softly in time with the patch's string lights and gets brighter as the patch bar fills. Hat and scarf are the cranberry accent. |
| **Moves (feel)** | Waddles, never runs: a slow roll, a belly-flop into a snow puff, a shake that sends sparkles. Idle is a gentle breathing sway; it yawns when the bar is low. When the bar is full it does a happy little spin and the star flares. At the fall it settles with a soft "poof" of flakes, never a crash. |
| **Move elements** | Mostly **frost** (a puff of flakes, a snowball-softly-thrown), one **light** move (the star twinkle, a dazzle), and one **helper** move (a snug scarf wrap that makes it, or a friend, a little cozier). Names are silly or sweet verbs, in the voice of *Giggle Drizzle* and *Rock-a-Bye*. |
| **When it is beaten** | It is "tuckered out", never hurt. It sits down, the star dims to a warm glow, and it yawns. At the fall (the shared bar full) it flops into a pillow of snow and everyone gets a thank-you. |
| **Where it comes from** | Christmas Eve, 16:00 patch-local, drawn by the lights and the snow. It dozes off at 10:00 on the 25th and wanders home if the bar is short. |

### Its tiny befriend form: **Bundlebit**

The same character at about the size of a Puddlepuff: one round head-body, the same ice-blue and snow-white, a **cranberry bobble hat** with a small lit bead (not a star, so the BIG one stays special), a stub of scarf, two dot eyes and a rosy blush. It is what joins your team ("Bundlebit joined your patch!"). It bounces on the spot and leaves a faint trail of flakes. Same element family: frost first, a light helper second.

## The frost squishies (one line each, for 3a and 3b to start from)

All frost-element and cute; each has a **seasonal reason to exist** (STYLE_GUIDE §4): they come near the lights, the tree and the snow. Colours are two plus an accent.

- **Jinglehoof**: a chubby little reindeer-pony in cream and ice-blue with a cranberry ribbon and a small **gold bell** that jingles when it trots; the signature prop is the bell.
- **Mittenmoth**: a fluffy moth whose wings are two **mittens** (white-blue, one cranberry cuff each); it is drawn to the porch lights and flutters in zig-zags.
- **Snowglobin**: a round, clear-blue squishy with a tiny snowy village swirling inside its belly; shake it and the flakes fall; the signature prop is the glass dome.
- **Tinselwisp**: a small floating wisp of silver-and-green tinsel with two bright eyes; it shimmers and twirls near the tree; the accent is a bit of red.
- **Cocoamallow**: a mug-shaped marshmallow in cocoa-brown and white, steaming gently, with a candy-cane handle; the cosiest of the lot.
- **Aurorelle** (Mythic, **iridescent**): a graceful flowing ribbon-like squishy whose body shifts through the northern-lights greens, pinks and violets over a pale frost base; the one frost squishy that glows from within, the rarest and calmest.
